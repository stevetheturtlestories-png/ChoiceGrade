How to use a guide with your quotes

1. Match the scope

Mark what every bid includes, excludes or leaves unclear. Check that all bidders priced the same work.

2. Ask the same questions

Use the email template in your project guide and request revised written quotes with the answers.

3. Compare the answers

Weigh the final scope, possible extras, warranties and terms before deciding whether a price difference is worth it.
